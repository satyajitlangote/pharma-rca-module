Title: Out-of-specification unknown impurity in Valsartan API final release testing

Quantity Affected: 6 drums (480 kg)
Immediate Containment: Batch VLS-26018 placed on QA hold; remaining drums of the drying campaign (VLS-26017) also placed on hold pending investigation.

1. SUMMARY
During release testing of batch VLS-26018 the unknown impurity by HPLC was 0.14 %
against the specification limit of NMT 0.10 % (specification SPEC-VAL-004, current
version 6). The result was confirmed on re-test from a second container on
2026-09-19 (OOS-26-0031 opened).

2. LABORATORY DATA
HPLC sequence SEQ-260919-A: blank, system suitability, samples.
Retention time of the unknown peak 8.7 min; peak area 0.14 % against reporting
threshold 0.05 %. Re-test from container 4 of drum 3: 0.13 %. Laboratory
investigation found no preparation error; glassware and balances were within
calibration (balance CAL-26-0655, due 2027-02-15).

3. BATCH MANUFACTURING RECORD REVIEW (BMR-26018)
The batch manufacturing record shows the drying step started on 2026-09-18 at
06:20 with a recipe setpoint of 55 C and a maximum of 55 C per SOP-PR-014.
Operator entries every 30 minutes record 55 C until 09:30. After 09:30 there are
no operator entries until 12:00. At 12:00 the entry reads 68 C and the remark
"alarm acknowledged, continuing".

4. SCADA TREND REVIEW
The SCADA trend export for dryer DR-07 on 2026-09-18 shows the product
temperature rising from 55 C at 09:35 to a peak of 71.4 C at 10:40 and staying
above 65 C until 12:02 (142 minutes above the 55 C limit). The steam valve
position signal SV-12 oscillates between 82 % and 100 % during the excursion.

5. EQUIPMENT AND MAINTENANCE RECORDS
Equipment logbook entry 2026-09-16: "SV-12 positioner not responding, reported
to maintenance." Maintenance work order WO-26-4471 for dryer DR-07 was opened
2026-09-16 and closed 2026-09-21 with the action "positioner replaced". The
preventive maintenance record for DR-07 shows the positioner was not included in
the quarterly PM checklist.

Temperature probe T-114 (dryer DR-07) calibration record CAL-26-0789 shows
calibration on 2026-07-02, next due 2027-01-02, result pass — the probe was in
calibration during the event.

6. PERSONNEL
Operator interview (2026-09-20, signed): "I saw the high temperature alarm and
acknowledged it because the batch was already long; I did not know I had to stop
drying." Training record shows operator R. Mehta trained on alarm response
(TR-26-0142, 2026-05-04) and on SOP-PR-014 (2026-05-06).

7. IMMEDIATE ACTIONS
- Batch remains on QA hold.
- Drying of subsequent batches suspended on dryer DR-07 until SV-12 is repaired
  and verified (work order WO-26-4471 closed 2026-09-21).
- 100 % re-test of affected drums planned after engineering assessment.

Note: no change control for the DR-07 control philosophy has been raised yet.
